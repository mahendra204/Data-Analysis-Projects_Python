## 📊 Projects Description

### 1. **Vrinda Store Data Analysis** (Root Folder)

**Objective:** Analyze sales and orders data to provide business insights for growth optimization.

**Data Source:** Internal business database

**Tools Used:** Python, Pandas, Pivot Tables, Excel Dashboard

**Files:**
- `hotel_booking.csv` - Booking data dataset
- `Vrinda Store Data Analysis project.xlsx` - Dashboard with slicers
- `Vrinda Store Data set.xlsx` - Original dataset

**Analysis Questions Addressed:**
1. Compare sales and orders using single chart
2. Identify highest sales and order months
3. Gender-based purchasing analysis (2022)
4. Order status distribution (2022)
5. Top 10 states by sales contribution
6. Age and gender relationship with orders
7. Channel contribution to sales
8. Top-selling product categories

**Key Insights:**
- **March** shows the highest sales and orders
- **Women buyers** dominate with higher purchase rates than men
- **Top states:** Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh drive maximum sales
- **Top channels:** Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra are primary sales channels
